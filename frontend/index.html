How group facilitation works

  • Call someone by name (e.g. “Jordan, what do you think?”) to get a guaranteed reply.
  • Say “Discuss together” to open the floor—students decide whether to speak.
  • Say “Jordan, please watch” to keep a student listening while others talk.
